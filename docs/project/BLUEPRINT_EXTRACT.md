1. Executive decision memo
   Concept: a managed marketplace for verified non-medical companions and practical assistants,
   bookable hourly, daily, weekly or on a recurring schedule. Buyers may book for themselves or for a
   consenting family member in another city. Supply is recruited, verified, trained, matched and
   supervised by the platform.
   Recommendation: pilot two or three high-trust categories in selected Delhi NCR neighborhoods
   before expanding to Bengaluru, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Kolkata and Ahmedabad. Do
   not promise all-metro coverage before reliable local operations. Build an operations-first business
   with software as an enabler.
   
   Beachhead: adult children aged 28–55 arranging support for independently living parents; also
   busy professionals needing hospital escorts.
   
   Launch offer: hospital appointment escort, elderly companionship/check-in, station pickup/drop
   (not full train journey initially).
   
   Core promise: verified person, clearly scoped task, visible progress, consent-based handover,
   responsive human escalation.
   
   Avoid in MVP: nursing, medicine administration, unsupervised child care, intimate personal
   care, emergency medical response, heavy lifting, overnight intercity travel.

Illustrative 4-hour hospital booking: customer service price ₹1,596 (₹399/h, excluding taxes), worker
payout ₹1,000, travel subsidy ₹80, payment costs ₹30, support/quality allocation ₹90, incident
reserve ₹35 => contribution ₹361 (22.6%) before marketing and fixed overhead. If blended
acquisition cost is ₹450, first-booking economics are negative by ₹89; repeats must recover
acquisition cost. This is a model, not a forecast.
At ₹361 contribution/booking and ₹6,00,000 monthly fixed cost, approximately 1,663 completed
bookings/month are required to cover fixed cost before additional growth investment, refunds,
income taxes and financing. At 30 bookings/day, contribution ₹3.25 lakh/month; at 60/day ₹6.50 ≈≈
lakh/month. Validate each input with actual city-level operations. 8. Launch strategy and metro sequencing
Phase 0 (weeks 1–4): validate in 2 Delhi NCR micro-markets, run manual WhatsApp/phone bookings,
build worker SOPs. Phase 1 (weeks 5–12): controlled Delhi NCR pilot; target 30 trained workers and
100–200 paid bookings. Phase 2 (months 4–6): improve retention and reliability; launch Bengaluru
or Pune only after positive contribution margin. Phase 3 (months 7–12): expand to Mumbai,
Hyderabad and other metros based on demand density and local leadership. Expansion order is a
hypothesis, not market-size ranking.

CONFIDENTIAL • Startup planning draft • October 2026 9. Trust, safety and operating procedures

Identity: document checks through compliant vendors, consent, human interview, two
references, police verification where available and lawful, periodic rechecks; a badge is not a
guarantee of safety.

Role-specific training: safeguarding older adults, respectful communication, consent, mobility
boundaries, de-escalation, basic first-aid awareness, incident escalation, privacy, anti-
harassment.

At booking: assess mobility, accessibility, language, gender preference when appropriate,
duration, authorized contacts, medical-risk exclusions and transport arrangements.

At arrival: verified photo/name, geofence, start PIN exchanged in person; never disclose
customer OTPs to worker remotely.

During service: consent-based location sharing, checkpoint updates, missed-check-in alerts, SOS
routed to staffed operations; use 112 for police/emergency assistance and 108/102 where locally
applicable for ambulance.

At completion: end PIN, proof of handover to named adult, receipts, customer rating, worker
safety check, dispute window.

Incidents: severity levels P0–P3; P0 immediate emergency-service call and supervisor escalation;

written logs, evidence retention limits, mandatory reporting as applicable.

Worker protections: transparent pay, reasonable travel and rest periods, safe-shift refusal,
grievance process, harassment prevention, insurance assessment and no unsafe task coercion. 10. Regulatory and legal diligence — obtain Indian counsel

Entity formation, founders agreement, equity vesting, trademark clearance, contracts with
workers and customers, insurance and local shop/establishment obligations.

India Digital Personal Data Protection Act and applicable rules/commencement: privacy notices,
consent, purpose limitation, data minimization, retention, rights handling, breach response,
cross-border processing as applicable.

Aadhaar: do not store full numbers or perform unauthorized authentication; use lawful identity


Platform worker classification, social-security/labor obligations, state-specific

contractors.

Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules where applicable, refunds, grievance officer,


Medical boundaries: never market non-medical assistants as nurses; assess clinical


Rail travel: railway access rules, passenger tickets for companions, boarding and station
restrictions, disability assistance coordination and travel insurance.

Payments: use licensed payment aggregator, compliant split-settlement solution and refund
ledger; do not claim escrow without legal/payment-provider support.

CONFIDENTIAL • Startup planning draft • October 2026 13. Recommended architecture (React Native + Node.js + MongoDB)
Frontend: React Native with TypeScript (Expo development builds if native modules permit);
separate customer and worker app targets with shared design system. Admin: Next.js TypeScript.
Backend: Node.js TypeScript with NestJS or Express/Fastify; modular monolith first, not
microservices. Data: MongoDB Atlas with geospatial indexes and transactions where needed; Redis
for queues/cache/locks; S3-compatible encrypted object storage for restricted documents. Payments:
India-supported gateway and compliant settlement product. Messaging: FCM/APNs plus
WhatsApp/SMS provider. Maps: Google Maps/Mapbox with consent-based tracking.
Infrastructure: Docker, GitHub Actions, managed cloud, separate dev/staging/prod, secret manager,
WAF/rate limiting, monitoring, structured logs, alerting, backups and restore drills. Avoid storing
Aadhaar images in ordinary application collections; encrypt sensitive documents and use expiring
signed URLs.

Website content reviewed 9 October 2026. No independent validation of live coverage, partner
counts, operational response, ratings, transaction completion or pricing checkout was performed.
Website-stated prices may differ from actual quotes. For every competitor, place a test inquiry in
Delhi, Bengaluru and Mumbai, document the final quote for a standard 4-hour hospital visit, check
refunds, ask for verification proof and compare the booking journey. Update this plan quarterly.
Final recommendation: win one high-stakes workflow with repeat use—hospital visits for elderly
parents—before expanding horizontally into generic household help. Your moat will be trust
operations, continuity, outcome documentation and dependable local supply, not React Native
features alone.
